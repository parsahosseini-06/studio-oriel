02 / Work · Case study 01

House on the Ridge

One long room, one thick wall, one window toward the valley.

Place
Fictional ridge town
Year
2024
Type
Residential
Role
Architecture and interiors
Area
186 m²
Client
Private

The site is a narrow ridge with a long view south and a cold wind from the north. We kept the house to one room deep, so every space gets light from two sides and nothing sits in the dark.

A thick concrete wall runs along the north edge. It holds the stair, the storage and the services, and it keeps the wind off the living space. Everything on the south side is thin and open.

A long window is cut toward the valley, set low so that a person sitting sees the slope and not the sky. The material is plain: concrete, oak and glass, each used once and left alone.

Detail: the north wall is 450 mm thick and holds the stair, storage and services.

Case study 02

Courtyard Library

A reading room around a quiet yard.

Place
Fictional river town
Year
2023
Type
Cultural
Role
Architecture
Area
1 240 m²

The library wraps a small courtyard, so each reading room looks into green and away from the street. Brick walls match the old town. Inside, long tables sit under deep windows, and the stair doubles as seating for talks.

04 / Philosophy

Begin with the window, end with the street.

  1. 01

    Start with the opening.

    Where light enters and what it frames shape a room before the plan does.

  2. 02

    Measure before you draw.

    We walk the site and write down distances, slopes and shadows first.

  3. 03

    One material, one idea.

    Each material does a single job and is left as it is.

  4. 04

    Finish at the street.

    A building is read from outside first, so we test every design from the public edge.
