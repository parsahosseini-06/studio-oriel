## 3. How architecture sites use editorial layout (observed from text + reasoned)
Observed in the fetched text: typed labels, short factual or evocative captions, project title plus place, numbered or ordered feeds, philosophy/process teasers on home.
Reasoned, not verified visually: architecture sites commonly use large imagery with generous whitespace and caption-led hierarchy.

## 4. Collage: design guidance (reasoned; no observed example)
- Treat collage as **composition, not decoration**: a few photos cropped, scaled and overlapped on a grid, with one dominant image and smaller supporting fragments (detail, texture, plan).
- Keep text on clear zones; collage must never lower contrast under type.
- With few licensed photos, vary crop and scale to get variety rather than adding assets.
- Use drawn or typographic elements (plan lines, labels, numbers) as connective tissue, which suits architecture.
- Keep a calm fallback: collage stays legible with motion off and on small screens (reduce overlap, stack).
- Avoid implying photos are the fictional studio's work: caption as "Image: photographer / Unsplash".

## 5. Journey moments where motion can carry meaning
| Moment | Motion purpose | Idea (no visual direction yet) |
|---|---|---|
| Arrive | Hierarchy, orientation | Collage fragments settle into place in order of importance, then stop; wordmark and statement read first |
| Understand | Continuity | Scroll reveals the intro in short steps; fragments shift slightly to stay related to the text |
| Trust | State, evidence | Philosophy principles appear one at a time; numbers/credits resolve quietly |
| Explore a project | Continuity, spatial logic | Project image expands from its collage thumbnail into the case study and back, so the user keeps their place |
| Decide | Hierarchy, feedback | Services highlight on hover/focus; CTA gains emphasis as the user nears the end |
| Contact | Feedback, state | Field focus, validation and "sent" confirmation animate briefly |
Rules: respect `prefers-reduced-motion`; durations short; animate transform/opacity only; no motion without one of the four purposes. Visual direction and the motion system belong to Phase 2 (Vera).
